# Tests for 2026-10-28

Part A covers leaf stress symptoms. Part B covers the reset-camera button, tree names and
falling-leaf performance, which were added later the same day. All of it is uncommitted until these pass. When you're done, tell Claude what happened
and update `Docs/CurrentLogs.txt` if anything looked wrong.

**What changed:** leaves now change colour when the tree is stressed, and badly stressed leaves
drop out of season. Each cause has its own colour, and some species drop leaves more readily than
others.

| Cause | Leaf colour |
|---|---|
| Drought | crisp tan |
| Waterlogged soil / root rot | yellow |
| Low nutrients | pale, even yellow-green |
| A failing branch | brown, on that branch only |
| Fungus | sickly yellow-green (unchanged) |

# Part A: Leaf stress symptoms

**New since yesterday:** every in-game week while the tree is stressed, the Console logs a
`[LeafStress]` line naming the cause and its strength (drought, waterlog, hunger, weak tips) and how
many leaves dropped. If anything looks wrong, those lines are what Claude needs: update
`CurrentLogs.txt`.

## Before you start

- Use a **broadleaf** species for tests 1–4. Japanese Maple is the best choice: it shows colour
  well and drops at a normal rate.
- Start a new tree, or load one that's at least a couple of years old and in **summer**
  (June–August), when it has a full set of leaves.
- **Settings ▸ Play Modes:** pick **Active Play** and untick **Auto-water**, **Auto-fertilize** and
  **Auto-style**.
- **Calendar:** delete any watering or fertilizer schedules, including moisture rules.
- Run at **Fast** speed so a day passes in seconds. Slow down when something starts to happen.
- Open the **◉ health stats** view so you can watch moisture and nutrients.

## 1. Drought

1. Leave the tree unwatered in summer and watch the moisture bar fall.
2. Once moisture drops below the species' drought line (about 30% for most species):
   - **Expected:** over about 4–5 in-game days, leaves shift from green to a dry tan, gradually,
     not all at once.
   - **Expected:** once they're well tan, leaves start falling, a few at a time, in midsummer.
3. Water it with the watering can (click the soil).
   - **Expected:** leaves stop falling straight away. The tan fades back to green slowly, over
     roughly two weeks of in-game time.
   - **Expected:** branch tips that lost all their leaves stay bare **while the tree is still
     stressed**. After about **10 calm in-game days**, if it's still before September, they push a
     **second flush** of new leaves. After August they wait for spring.
   - **Expected:** the first time, a **"Drought Stress"** tip explains the cause and fix. After
     that, a short red toast appears instead, at most once every 20 in-game days.

**Report if:** nothing changes colour, all the leaves snap to tan in one jump, they turn brown
instead of tan, leaves never drop, or the whole canopy falls within a day or two.

## 2. Failing branch

This usually shows up during test 1, because a long drought damages branch health.

- **Expected:** some branches go a darker **brown** while the rest stay tan or green. The brown is
  per branch, not the whole tree.
- Repotting also stresses branches. Try a repot in summer and watch for a few brown branches
  afterwards.

**Report if:** the brown appears on the whole tree at once, or never appears even after a long
drought.

## 3. Low nutrients

This one takes in-game years, so use Fast speed.

1. With auto-fertilize off and no fertilizer schedule, let **two springs** pass. Each spring
   uses up nutrients.
2. Once nutrients drop below 30% in the health stats:
   - **Expected:** leaves turn a **pale, even yellow-green** across the whole tree. It looks washed
     out rather than brown.
3. Fertilize.
   - **Expected:** colour slowly returns.

**Report if:** the pale colour never shows up even with nutrients near zero.

## 4. Waterlogging (yellow)

**Changed after the second play-test:** saturation now rises and falls **day by day**. Before, rain
pushed it up daily but it only came down once a year, so one wet spring left the pot waterlogged
for a whole year with nothing the player could do. You can watch it in the **repot panel**
("Saturation: N%").

1. Repot into a **Moist** substrate if you can. It holds water and drains poorly.
2. Keep the soil soaking wet: in the **Calendar**, add a watering rule with the **Moisture** option
   set to **90%**.
3. Run at Fast speed for a few weeks to a couple of months. A rainy spell speeds it up.
   - **Expected:** saturation climbs past 50%, leaves turn **yellow**, and a **"Waterlogged Soil"**
     tip explains the cause and fix.
   - **Expected:** with saturation near 100%, leaves start dropping.
4. Delete the rule and let the soil dry to about **50%** before watering again.
   - **Expected:** saturation falls over roughly **1–2 in-game weeks**, faster in a free-draining
     mix. The yellow eases with it, and bare tips push a second flush after about 10 calm days
     (before September).
5. Repeat with a normal mix and a watering rule at about **45–50%**.
   - **Expected:** an occasional rainy spell may yellow the leaves briefly, but it clears on its
     own. No season-long dying.

**Report if:**
- saturation never falls while the soil is drying;
- it still goes yellow all summer on a normal mix with sensible watering;
- a whole season of soaking produces no yellow at all.

## 5. Species differences

Repeat **test 1 (drought)** on two contrasting species:

- **Ficus** should drop leaves **early and heavily**. It's the most sensitive species.
- **Juniper** or a **pine** should brown but **mostly hold** its needles.

**Report if:** the two behave about the same.

## 6. Quick check: nothing breaks

- **Autumn:** normal autumn colour and leaf fall still happen as before on a healthy tree.
- **Quick Start:** generate a 10-year tree. It should come out with a normal canopy, not half-bare.
- **Reduced motion** (Settings ▸ Accessibility): stress-dropped leaves fall straight down too.

# Part B: Reset camera, tree names, leaf performance

## 7. Reset camera

1. Orbit, zoom and pan somewhere awkward, then click the new **🎥** button. It's at the top of the
   right-hand column, above 📅.
   - **Expected:** the camera snaps back to the angle the game opened with, framed so the whole
     tree fits.
2. Press **R**. It should do the same thing.
3. Try it on a **seedling** and on a **big Quick Start tree**.
   - **Expected:** both are framed sensibly, not lost in the distance or cropped.
4. Turn on cinematic mode (C), then press R.
   - **Expected:** cinematic mode stops and the camera resets.
5. **Settings ▸ Controls:** "Reset the camera" is listed and can be rebound.

**Report if:** the 🎥 icon shows as a box (it would need a different symbol), or the framing is
too tight or too loose.

## 8. Tree names

1. Start a new game. After picking a species, the Play Modes screen has a **TREE NAME** field at the
   top. Type a name and press START.
   - **Expected:** the HUD date line reads **"Name · March 3, 2027 07:00"**.
2. **Settings ▸ Game:** the same field is at the top. Rename the tree.
   - **Expected:** the HUD updates as you type.
3. Save, quit to the main menu, and open **Play / Load**.
   - **Expected:** the save card shows **"Name · Species"**.
4. Load it. The name should come back.
5. Load an **old save** from before this change.
   - **Expected:** no name, no errors, and the card shows just the species.
6. Clear the name.
   - **Expected:** the HUD goes back to the date alone.

## 9. Falling leaves: performance

1. Open **Window ▸ Analysis ▸ Profiler** or the **Stats** overlay in the Game view, and note the
   frame time on a mature broadleaf tree in **summer**.
2. Run into **autumn** at Fast speed and watch the frame time while the colours turn and leaves
   fall.
   - **Expected:** much less of a drop than before, especially while colours are changing.
3. **Look:** the autumn colour change should still look smooth.
   - It now repaints in 100 small steps instead of every frame.
   - Falling leaves keep the colour they dropped with.
   - Leaves disappear once they've fallen well below where they let go, about 3 units, which
     should be under the table and out of shot.

**Report if:**
- colours visibly step instead of fading smoothly;
- leaves pop out of existence while still visible on screen (the 3-unit cutoff is too short);
- autumn still stutters (send a Profiler screenshot of the worst frame).

# Part C: Care improvements (added after the play-tests)

## 10. Opening
- New game: the small pot shows straight away. The seed is buried. The opening tip says to pick
  up the can and click the soil; clicking the soil plants it.
- The first two days run at Medium, then drop to Slow. The sprout barely sways until its first
  leaves open.

## 11. Moisture bar
- A **white tick** on the moisture bar marks the species' drought line (40% on a maple). The bar
  is red below it, amber just above it and blue when well watered.
- A **thin purple bar** under it shows waterlogging. It turns a deeper purple past 50%.

## 12. Pouring
- With the can, a **click** adds a little (about 8%). **Holding** keeps pouring at about 45% a
  second. The can animates while pouring.
- Calendar rules and auto-water still give a full soak.

## 13. Forecast and shelter
- Under the AP bar: today's weather and the **next three days**. Check that the forecast comes
  true as the days pass.
- **Shelter** button (right column, above 🎥): it turns blue and reads **Covered**.
  - **Expected:** no rain reaches the pot and the saturation bar stops climbing in a storm.
  - **Expected:** sway is reduced and the tree grows more slowly.
  - **Expected:** saving and loading keeps the shelter on.

## 14. Inspect
- With **no tool**, click a leaf. A card by the pointer says what's wrong with that branch and
  what to do, or "Healthy" with its health and the soil moisture.
- Dragging to orbit the camera shouldn't pop a card.

## 15. Recovery
- After a stress warning, fix the problem. About 10 calm in-game days later:
  - **Expected:** a gold-bordered "Your tree has recovered" banner and +15 AP.
  - **Expected:** before September, bare tips re-leaf and thinned tips fill back up to a full
    cluster.
- The stress tips (Waterlogged / Drought / Hungry) show again on every new game and every load.

## 16. Fixes from the fourth play-test
- **Ficus is now evergreen.** In autumn it keeps its leaves: no autumn colour, no bare winter.
- **A / D tool cycling:** every tool now names itself on the HUD date line. Pressing **A** with no
  tool wraps round to **Jin (deadwood)**, which has no button. Before this fix it showed no name
  while still killing the branch you clicked. **Promote** was unnamed too.
- **Inspect card:** larger text (16px, up from 13px).

## 17. Saw and the clipper thickness limit
- Select the trimmers and click a **thick** branch or the trunk. "Thick" is relative to this tree:
  at least **35% of the trunk's base radius** (on a 20-year tree, about the thickness of a main
  branch). A seedling never needs the saw.
  - **Expected:** nothing is cut. A red banner says it's too thick and to press D until the tool reads
    Saw. The same happens with the big clippers.
- Press **D** to the Saw and click the same branch. It stays highlighted while you saw.
  - Move the mouse **back and forth across the branch** (perpendicular to it as it looks on
    screen). Moving along the branch does nothing. On a horizontal branch that means up and down.
  - **Expected:** a rasp with each stroke, sawdust drifting down from the cut, and a few splinters
    flicked out at each change of direction. About 5 full back-and-forths cut it through.
- Start a saw cut, then press **Esc** or right-click: the highlight clears and the branch is untouched.
- Thin twigs: the clippers still cut them in one click, and so does the saw.

**Report if:** sawdust shows as pink or black squares, the sound is harsh or silent, strokes across
the branch don't register, or the limit catches branches
that look thin. Each refusal and each saw start logs a `[Saw]` line with the branch radius and the
limit. Tune **Saw Trunk Fraction** (0.35) and **Saw Min Radius** (0.02) on the tree's TreeSkeleton
component.

## If something's off

The amounts are adjustable in the Inspector without code changes:

- **LeafManager** component on the tree, under *Stress symptoms*:
  - how fast colour comes on (`Stress Onset Per Day`, 0.12)
  - how fast it fades (`Stress Recovery Per Day`, 0.04)
  - the point where leaves start dropping (`Stress Drop Threshold`, 0.55)
  - the drop rate (`Stress Drop Per Day`, 0.10)
- **Each species asset:** `Stress Leaf Drop` (Ficus 2.0 … Juniper 0.2).

Note any values you try, so the good ones can be made permanent.
